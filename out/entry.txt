At six the cobbler pulls the shop door shut behind him, and the bell over the door swings on, and on, and on.

The swing narrows to the width of a hand, and its note is full. The swing narrows to two fingers, and the note has less in it. The swing narrows to a line, and the note has no ring in it.

The note goes.

To nothing.

A cat sleeps on the counter. Its chin lowers onto its paws a little at a time. It rests there, and does not lift again.

The shoe sits finished, and the wax is warm where his thumb pressed it. The clock over the door goes on past the hour, tick by tick.

The last light.

The street lets go of its light, and the dark takes the glass. The shine on it holds, and holds. Nothing needs to be done for it to keep.

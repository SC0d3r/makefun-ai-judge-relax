Rain lands on the canvas in drops a hand apart, and the bag holds you as someone left it, zipped shut from the outside, warm.

A coat that is not yours lies over the bag, and a flask sits by your knee, set down warm. Someone did that before the rain. The torch stands by the bag, its hand of light out on the canvas.

The sound keeps the same beat as the drops. It drums while they land a hand apart. It taps when they come two fingers apart. The taps get smaller, and the sky slows drop by drop.

A line apart.

Rain is down to that now, one drop, then a long gap, and the sound ticks. The light on the canvas shrinks from a hand to two fingers, then to a line. The moon moves off the canvas, and the hour goes by with no clock. The ticks run out, and the sound is gone. Your hand opens. It stays open.

The light goes.

The dark lies on your side of the canvas now, and no one needs you before morning.

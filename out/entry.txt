Late light comes in low through the west window. It lies across the floor in a long yellow band.

The band touches the leg of a wooden chair and climbs it slowly. The grain of the wood shows in the light, warm and close.

A woman sits in the chair with her hands loose in her lap. Her shoes are off, set side by side on the rug, one heel against the other.

The light moves up the arm of the chair and onto the blanket folded there. The wool is pale and soft. It holds the color and goes quiet.

The band leaves the floor. It climbs the wall in a low stripe and stops under the window.

Her foot slides back under the chair without her deciding to. Then she is still. Her head rests against the chair back, her hands loose in her lap, and the chair is warm at her back.
